For clients, before we begin

What I would want you to read before, not after.

You do not need to read any of this to work with me. But most of the people I sit with are the kind who want to see the evidence themselves, so here it is, including the parts that complicate the story.

Start here: how this actually works

Oregon built the first state regulated psilocybin program in the country. Knowing the mechanics takes most of the fear out of it.

Oregon Health Authority: how to access psilocybin services Oregon Health Authority The official walkthrough. Twenty one and over, no prescription, no diagnosis, no referral. Preparation, then administration at a licensed service center, then integration. It also lists every form you will sign, including the Client Bill of Rights.
InnerTrek Where I trained Founded by Tom Eckert, who architected Measure 109. If you want to know what my training actually consisted of, it is described here in more detail than I could give you in a conversation.

What the research shows

Inaugural year of regulated psilocybin services in Oregon: safety, motivations, and utilization Frontiers in Psychiatry  ·  May 2026 5,935 clients and 5,375 sessions in 2025. Serious adverse events were rare: 2.42 behavioral and 2.79 medical per 1,000 sessions, and seven hospitalizations across the whole state for the whole year. If you want one number to hold, that is the one.
Psilocybin services and mental health outcomes within Oregon's state-regulated model Gow, Carhart-Harris, Davis et al.  ·  February 2026, preprint The first outcomes study conducted inside this exact model rather than in a lab. 88 participants, significant improvement in depression, anxiety, and well-being at thirty days, including among people who stayed on their psychiatric medication. Still a preprint, still small, still worth your time.
COMP360 psilocybin for treatment resistant depression, phase 3 Compass Pathways  ·  six month data, July 2026 581 participants. Thirty nine percent of the 25 mg group reached a clinically meaningful drop in depression scores by week six, and the effect held through week twenty six. Read it knowing this is a controlled clinical trial, not a service center. The setting is different and the difference is not cosmetic.
Single-dose psilocybin for major depressive disorder: a randomized clinical trial JAMA  ·  2023 One of the studies that moved this out of the fringe. Good if you want to see the method rather than the headline.

The part most people skip

If someone only shows you the encouraging research, be careful with them.

Growing scope of Oregon's psilocybin program raises safety concerns Johns Hopkins, International Journal of Drug Policy  ·  June 2026 Most people using Oregon's program are seeking wellness rather than treatment, and they skew high income and out of state. The researchers raised real concern about people bringing serious psychiatric conditions into a supported adult use model that was not designed to treat them. This is the study behind how carefully I screen, and behind the fact that I will tell some people no.

What none of this research can tell you is whether it will work for you. The honest position is that the evidence is real, growing, and still early, and that the container matters as much as the compound. Anyone who tells you more than that is selling you something.
